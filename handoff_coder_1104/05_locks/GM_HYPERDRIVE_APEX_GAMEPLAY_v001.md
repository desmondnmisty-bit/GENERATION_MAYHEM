**Product:** Generation Mayhem: The Bloodline / Two Arena Death Derby  
**Visual lock:** `concepts/apex_hyperdrive_1103/GM_Apex_Hyperdrive_Hero_CityLivedIn_v003.jpg` — city-set, worn, lived-in; cyan/purple kept; **no moon**; not outer space.

## Layout
1. **Round base** at bottom with **little walls** up as lived-in working areas / spots.
2. **Tunnel** up inside the round section → **middle dome**.
3. **Elevator / doohickey shaft** = breather between Pink Viper fight and top.
4. **Top** = final arena.

## Combat route
1. Fight **Pink Vipers** from start all the way to a **doorway**.
2. At doorway: **player cannot act** — cut to **scripted movie**: characters go up in elevator → doors open at top → **Slither waiting** → fade out → resume gameplay at top for Slither fight.
3. **Top fight = Slither only** — no other big threat.
4. Fight does **not** need to be long: good hard portion where players get their asses kicked → **Slither gets away** → nobody really wins → players worse for wear, ready for next.
5. **Bubble/dome mechanism** lets Slither **disappear from the water area** so he doesn’t get knocked off.

## Priority mechanics (above everything else in this level)
1. **Shatter Slingshot** — big-deal look / feel / playability (more than current). Roster: player **Vixen** plants/anchors; **Kailee** launches; Brianna/Payton do not use Shatter.
2. **Sonic resonator / sonic muzzle scream** — big deal: player does slingshot and as Kailee comes around, **player screams** and it makes Kailee go **~200× faster**. Sonic scream + Shatter = the two biggest mechanics — prioritize look, feel, playability.
