HOW TO PLAY

THE GOAL

Skate north through the night dockyard. Survive 4 waves of Pink Vipers and the Iron Rose Syndicate. Each cleared wave drops the magenta laser fence ahead. Beat Falcon at the last berth, then skate into the EXTRACTION GATE to win.

KEYBOARD & MOUSE

  • W A S D skate. You have momentum, so accelerate, carve and glide
  • MOUSE aim the camera (click the game to lock the mouse; Esc releases it)
  • SPACE / SHIFT brake · follow the green arrow to your next objective
  • LEFT CLICK / J Soft handgun SHOOT (pistol shots, ~0.3s cooldown)
  • RIGHT CLICK / K sonic cone (short-range blast, staggers everything)
  • E / L SHATTER-SLINGSHOT (when Chaos is full)
  • F / Q partner CHECK: when Fang's PINK POUNCE pins you, Kailee body-checks her off. If Kailee is pinned, press J near Fang to check her off
  • ARROW KEYS turn the camera without the mouse
  • ESC / P pause · M mute

TOUCH

Left stick to skate. Use the SHOOT, SCREAM and SLING buttons. II pauses.

KAILEE & THE CHAOS METER

Kailee (the Striker) fights at your side with flaming wrist blades and her pearl .40. When she takes damage, Vixen's CHAOS meter fills. If Kailee goes down, she gets back up after 8 seconds with 25% health.

SHATTER-SLINGSHOT

When Chaos is full, the screen shakes, a cue sounds and the meter flashes. Aim at a line of enemies and press E. Vixen tethers to Kailee and screams through the Resonator Muzzle, and the sonic blast launches Kailee straight through them for massive damage and knockback.

DOCK HAZARDS

  • CRANE HOOK a swinging container load sweeps across berth 2. Time your pass or skate around it.
  • ELECTRIFIED PUDDLES crackle on and off. Skate around them or wait until they're dark. Enemies get fried too.
  • WATER'S EDGE skate off the quay (past the yellow hazard curb) and you go in. You respawn, but it costs health. Knock enemies in for an instant KO.
