## The Lore (start → end)
**Vixen** was a star fighter for the **Iron Rose Syndicate** for a decade. She retired, married, and built a family. Her husband died.

Her daughters — **Brianna, Kailee, and Payton** — make dumb decisions and get forced into **blood contracts**, playing the **Death Derby** to pay off debts to Iron Rose and their hired goons: **IRS brutes** and the **Pink Vipers**.

Vixen gets the phone call that they have contracted her kids. Rage. Kitchen destroyed. Garage. Dusty cabinet. **War Rig**. She straps in and comes out of retirement — **Mama Bear**, fighting to protect her legacy.

**Cold open (cutscene movie):** pregnant + hubby → births of Brianna, Kailee, Payton → quick happy life flashes → husband dies → devastated → OK / moving on → Payton’s girls Kamyrn + Lili (emotion) → blood contracts / phone call → kitchen RAGE → garage cabinet → dusty War Rig → strap in → **CAN YOU SURVIVE THE CIRCUIT.**

In-match: Kamyrn + Lili = coms only. Jessica ≠ daughter.
**Cutscene wardrobe:** same faces as the roster — **normal daily wear only**, never arena kits / War Rig gear in the life-flash.


## Vertical Slice Arena — The Dockyard
Rusted multi-tier shipping hub → lethal combat track: crane bottlenecks, oil spills, container corridors, open asphalt. Family vs **Slither** (Pink Vipers boss) + squad. Win = knock Slither into the harbor (he returns). Lose = whole family dead/disabled.
